FAQ

Which Android versions work?

Android 10 (API 29) and newer.

The accessibility switch is grayed out.

On Android 13 and newer, open Settings, Apps, AUREX, the three-dot menu, and choose Allow restricted settings. Then enable the service again.

Can it detect the power button?

No. Android does not allow apps to detect it, so AUREX doesn’t claim to.

Why does it need the accessibility service?

It is the only supported way for an app to see physical volume-button presses and draw over other apps.
